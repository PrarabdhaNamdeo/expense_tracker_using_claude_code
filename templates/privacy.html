Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Spendly handles your personal information and financial data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your name and email address. Additionally, we collect the financial transaction data you enter, including amounts, categories, and descriptions, to enable the core functionality of the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide personal finance tracking services. We use it to generate spending reports, calculate totals, and help you analyze your financial habits. We do not use your data for marketing purposes or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Your data is stored securely on our servers and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with any third-party services. We may use basic infrastructure providers (like database hosting), but they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
